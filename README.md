## About

I design and verify digital hardware. Currently specializing in **Design Verification** and **RTL/ASIC Front-End** through the K-VLSI PG Diploma at IIIT Bangalore, where I work hands-on with SystemVerilog, UVM, and AMBA protocol verification.

Previously completed a research internship at **IIT Bombay** on 5G Core Network Function security validation. My work sits at the intersection of RTL design, protocol-level verification, and clock domain crossing — areas where correctness is non-negotiable.

**Currently open to:** Entry-level Design Verification Engineer and RTL/ASIC Front-End roles.
